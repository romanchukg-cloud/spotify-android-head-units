🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.12

A new interface of its own, made for the car!

• Home, Library, Search and pages for playlists, albums, artists and podcasts, with big buttons
• Player and mini-player on blurred cover art; swipe up to open the player
• Queue over the player: song and artist radio, Jam and a sleep timer
• Download progress, with green marks on downloaded songs
• Works in split screen next to navigation and in portrait
