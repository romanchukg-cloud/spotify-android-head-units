Our own interface

Home, Recents, Browse, Library and Search, with pages for playlists, albums, artists and podcasts and large touch targets. Navigation at the side, or along the bottom in portrait.

Smooth motion

While a page loads, placeholders in its shape shimmer instead of a “Loading…” line; then cards and rows rise in, covers fade in, pages slide in from the side and tabs fade through. The navigation highlight slides to the tab you tap.

Blurred cover art

The player, the mini-player and page headers sit on their cover, blurred. The controls stay on the driver's side; swipe the mini-player up to open the player, and swipe left or right on it or on the player to switch tracks. The mini-player shows the time and has shuffle, repeat and the devices too; the navigation closes the full-screen player.

Right-hand drive

Head unit settings → Steering wheel: Auto, Left or Right. On BYD, Auto asks the car which seat is the driver's; with right-hand drive the navigation, the controls and the microphone move to the right.

Devices

See where the music plays and move it: the player and the mini-player have a devices button — this car, your phone, computer or speakers with the same account. While another device plays, Spotify says so in green.

Home-screen widgets

Two Spotify widgets for launchers that let you add widgets: a strip, squares or a large player with the cover, times and controls, depending on their size, in day and night colours. BYD's own launcher shows only BYD's widgets.

Links and other apps

Spotify links that other apps open (spotify:, open.spotify.com, spotify.link) show the playlist, album or artist, or play the song. Launchers, scrobblers and head-up apps can see what is playing (a setting).

Queue and sleep timer

The queue opens over the player: play or remove tracks, start song or artist radio, or open Jam. The moon button pauses the music after 15–60 minutes or at the end of the track.

Downloads with progress

Download playlists and albums to listen offline (Spotify Premium). A green arrow marks downloaded songs, and the page counts them while it downloads. Experimental.

Free, from this page only

This app is free. If someone sold it to you, you were misled — ask for your money back. Download it only from this page. It is unofficial, not made by Spotify or BYD. A changed copy says so and gets no updates.

Split screen and portrait

The interface reaches the edges of its half of the screen next to navigation, as other apps do, and fits portrait screens; in a portrait split screen the player keeps a large cover. In landscape the keyboard lies over the interface instead of squeezing it.

Search

Results while you type, grouped like Spotify's, with recent searches. A microphone appears when the head unit has a speech recogniser; on BYD DiLink use the steering-wheel voice button or the keyboard's microphone.

One APK

The interface and the music service run within a single installed package — with no code from the BYD player app any more, and 10 MB smaller.

QR sign-in

Tap LOG IN, scan the code with your phone, and confirm your Spotify sign-in.

Accounts in settings

Open Head unit settings → Accounts to add an account with QR sign-in, rename saved accounts or switch between them.

Stays signed in

A pinned device ID, safe removal of old accounts and one automatic restore per boot keep Spotify signed in. Head unit settings show the last sign-in event.

Updates in the app

Spotify checks for new test builds daily and installs them after you confirm Android's Update window. Head unit settings → Updates has the switches and buttons. Builds older than test.9 need one manual install.

Playback startup

Head unit settings has separate options for resuming after boot or a long sleep and for playing on a new Spotify launch. Playback waits for the player to be ready; pause cancels automatic retries. It can also start Spotify in the background when the head unit starts (not on BYD, where switching the car off stops apps).

BYD ambient music rhythm

Spotify starts and stops the car’s own lighting rhythm with playback. Enable Music rhythm in the vehicle’s ambient-light settings; the car controls colours and effects.

Support and diagnostics

Preview and send a technical report, track or cancel pending reports, and export a ZIP. Automatic reporting is optional after the first-use notice.

Support and diagnostics

Open Head unit settings → Support. Send diagnostics lets you preview approved technical data before sending. Reports go to our shared server and a limited summary may appear in this project’s PUBLIC GitHub Issues. Automatically send diagnostics is selected on the first-use notice and starts only after Continue; you can turn it off in Support. Automatic reports cover measured integration failures only, at most one new report per day. Manual sending, pending status/cancellation and ZIP export remain available with automatic sending off. Reports exclude account data, song/playlist names, location and screenshots. Technical metadata is not a guarantee of anonymity.

What has been tested

Headunit-test.17 makes loading and moving around smooth: placeholders in the shape of what comes instead of “Loading…”, content rising in, covers fading in, pages sliding in and tabs fading through. Since test.16 it shows where the music plays and moves it between this car and your other devices (Spotify Connect); the mini-player has the time, shuffle, repeat and the devices, the screen a thin frame, and the navigation closes the full-screen player. Since test.14 it also has home-screen widgets for launchers that let you add widgets (five layouts by size, day and night), opens Spotify links from other apps, tells other apps what is playing and can start Spotify in the background when the head unit starts (not on BYD). It was made with the full reference build. The release audit reports 52 PASS / 0 FAIL; 152 updater, 39 sign-in, 14 panel-access, 13 Connect-message and 19 playback-start checks pass, and the signing certificate equals test.16. On Android 9 and 13 emulators test.17 installs as an update over test.16 and runs its update check without a crash; the sign-in and Accounts flow passes on Android 13. The widgets were checked in the stock launchers of Android 11 and 12 emulators; links opened playlists, albums and artists there. A build with the same code runs in a BYD Tang (DiLink 5, Android 12), where the new motion was checked; moving the music to a laptop and back was checked there with test.16.

Experimental headunit-test.17. Moving the music was checked with a laptop only. The widgets have shown only made-up tracks so far, and BYD's own launcher cannot show them. A song link playing and a real app reading what is playing are unverified. Right-hand drive has not been seen on a right-hand-drive car; DiLink 5.1 / Android 13 and other head units remain unverified; Arabic was checked on an emulator only. The panel block appears only in apps that support it. Spotify keeps downloads off for its Automotive client; this build turns the client's support on, so Spotify may change how downloads behave.

Android 9+ (API 28). BYD rhythm is verified on DiLink 5 / Android 12; DiLink 5.1 / Android 13 and other firmware are not yet verified. AAOS and GENERIC profiles are implemented; real head units from other manufacturers and AAOS remain unverified.

How to install

  1. Use the download button above to get the APK, then transfer it to the infotainment system.
  2. Update our previous test build with the same signature without uninstalling it: your sign-in may be preserved. Builds signed with a different key cannot be updated this way.
  3. Open Spotify and sign in using the QR code if needed.
  4. For BYD ambient lighting, enable Music rhythm in the car’s own ambient-light settings. This switch is in the vehicle settings, not in Spotify.
  5. To add another driver: Head unit settings → Accounts → Add account · QR. Switching stops playback and restarts the player.
  6. Check music playback and controls. Remove the old separate BYD interface only after the new APK passes these checks.
