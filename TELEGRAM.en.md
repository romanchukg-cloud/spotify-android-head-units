🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.16

Switch the music between your car, phone and computer!

• Devices (Spotify Connect): the player and the mini-player have a devices button. See where the music plays and move it with a tap — this car, your phone, computer or speakers with the same account. While another device plays, Spotify shows it in green; tap "This car" to bring the music back.
• Mini-player: shuffle, repeat and the devices button, as in the full-screen player.

⚠️ This app is free. If someone sold it to you, you were misled — ask for your money back. Download it only from the official page below. It is unofficial, not made by Spotify or BYD.

How to update: on test.9–test.15, Spotify offers test.16 inside the app, or open Head unit settings → Updates → Check. On older builds, install it once by hand as an update over your current build (don't uninstall).
