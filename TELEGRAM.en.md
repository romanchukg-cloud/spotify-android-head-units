🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.18

Spotify in Waze.

• Open Waze's audio panel (the music button), pick Spotify and accept Waze's "Connect to Spotify" once.
• Waze then shows what is playing with its controls and opens Home, Recents, Browse and Library without leaving the map.
• The heart beside the track adds it to Liked Songs; shuffle is there too where Spotify allows it (for example in Liked Songs).

⚠️ This app is free. If someone sold it to you, you were misled — ask for your money back. Download it only from the official page below. It is unofficial, not made by Spotify or BYD.

How to update: on test.9–test.17, Spotify offers test.18 inside the app, or open Head unit settings → Updates → Check. On older builds, install it once by hand as an update over your current build (don't uninstall).
