🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.15

Home-screen widgets, Spotify links, the time in the mini-player and more!

• Home-screen widgets: two Spotify widgets with the cover, the time and previous / play / next. They adapt to their size — a strip, a square or a large player — in day and night colours. They need a launcher that lets you add widgets: BYD's own launcher shows only BYD's widgets.
• Spotify links: links from other apps (spotify:, open.spotify.com, spotify.link) open the playlist, album or artist in Spotify, or play the song.
• What's playing: launchers, scrobblers and head-up apps that support Spotify can now see the current song (Head unit settings → Show other apps what is playing).
• The mini-player shows the time, and tapping the navigation closes the full-screen player.
• Start with the head unit: Spotify can start in the background when the head unit starts (Head unit settings, off by default). Not on BYD — switching the car off stops apps.

⚠️ This app is free. If someone sold it to you, you were misled — ask for your money back. Download it only from the official page below. It is unofficial, not made by Spotify or BYD.

How to update: on test.9–test.14, Spotify offers test.15 inside the app, or open Head unit settings → Updates → Check. On older builds, install it once by hand as an update over your current build (don't uninstall).
