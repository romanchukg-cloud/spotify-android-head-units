🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.17

Smoother everywhere: loading, pages and the player.

• Loading: instead of a "Loading…" line, you see placeholders shaped like what is coming — rows of cards, playlists, tracks, search results. Then the cards and rows gently rise in.
• Covers fade in instead of popping up; when the track changes, the new cover and background blend in over the old ones.
• Pages slide in from the side and back returns the way it came; tabs change with a soft fade, and the highlight in the navigation slides to the tab you tap.
• The full-screen player rises smoothly and closes faster.
