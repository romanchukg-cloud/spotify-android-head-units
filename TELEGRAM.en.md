🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.10

A new look for Spotify in your car!

• New style, on by default: a redesign closer to today's Spotify. Rounded cards, the Figtree typeface, day and night themes that follow the car, rounded app corners, and the mini-player at the bottom of the content area. Prefer the old look? Head unit settings → Appearance → Classic.
• Colour from cover art: the mini-player and the player take the colour of the current cover. It has its own switch and is on by default.
• Player: the controls come first, then like, cover and title. The like button now works.
• Queue: the track that is playing shows its cover with an animated equaliser.
• Library: a Settings button (gear) replaces "Your Library", so Head unit settings are easy to find.
• New Head unit settings screen. Closing Spotify now always stops the music.
• Fix: switching between day and night with Library open no longer crashes the player.

How to update: on test.9, Spotify offers test.10 inside the app, or open Head unit settings → Updates → Check. On older builds, install it once by hand as an update over your current build (don't uninstall).
