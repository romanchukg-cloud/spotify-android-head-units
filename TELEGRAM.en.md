🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.13

Swipes, right-hand drive and an interface edge to edge!

• Swipe to switch tracks: swipe left on the player or the mini-player for the next track, right for the previous one.
• Right-hand drive: Head unit settings → Steering wheel. On BYD, Auto asks the car where the driver sits; with right-hand drive the navigation and the controls move to the right.
• Edge to edge: the interface now reaches the status bar, the dock and the split-screen divider, like other apps. In a portrait split screen the player keeps a large cover.
• A Spotify widget for other apps' panels — Google Maps for BYD will be the first.
• No code from the BYD player app any more: 10 MB smaller.

⚠️ This app is free. If someone sold it to you, you were misled — ask for your money back. Download it only from the official page below. It is unofficial, not made by Spotify or BYD.

How to update: on test.9–test.12, Spotify offers test.13 inside the app, or open Head unit settings → Updates → Check. On older builds, install it once by hand as an update over your current build (don't uninstall).
