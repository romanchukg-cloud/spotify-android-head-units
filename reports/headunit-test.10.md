## How it was built

The New style changes the interface resources, which `adapter/incremental_release.py` keeps byte-identical, so test.10 comes from the full reference build `adapter/build.sh` with the prepared local inputs, signed with the release key. `restyle.py` runs after `patch_resources.py` and generates the New style as `car` UI-mode resources; the `# Restyle` section of `patch_headunit.py` adds the like-action alias and `MainActivity.getResources()`. Classic keeps the BYD-derived interface unchanged.

## Implemented in this APK

See [adapter/README.md](../adapter/README.md#test10-classic-and-new-style) for the design.

- **New style, on by default:** rounded cards with a 6 dp gap on a darker (night) or greyer (day) ground, the Figtree typeface (SIL Open Font License, `adapter/fonts/figtree`), larger radii, rounded app corners concentric with the cards, and the mini-player as the content card's footer.
- **Colour from cover art:** a separate switch, on by default; the mini-player and the player panel take a contrast-limited colour of the current cover.
- **Player and mini-player:** controls first, then like, cover and title. Spotify Automotive publishes its like actions as `like`/`unlike`; the interface now accepts them, so the like button appears.
- **Queue:** the playing row shows the cover with an animated equaliser.
- **Library:** the "Your Library" header became a Settings button with a gear that opens Head unit settings.
- **Head unit settings:** a new screen in the New style (Appearance, Spotify playback, Accounts, Updates, Support, Spotify account); Support, the diagnostics notice and Accounts follow the New style.
- **Stop on close:** closing Spotify always stops the music; the setting was removed from both styles.
- **Fix:** `ParcelBridge.getParcelableArrayList` no longer casts SavedStateHandle keys to `Parcelable`; recreating the player with Library open (for example on a day/night switch) crashed since test.3.

## In the car

On 2026-10-16 the build was installed on the owner's car, BYD DiLink 5.0 / Android 12, with `adb install -r` over test.9, without uninstalling.

- The installed `base.apk` has exactly the release SHA-256 `ccf440e3…f806`, versionCode **95487**; the package UID and the first-install date (2026-10-09) were kept.
- Sign-in, the library and playback were kept after the update. The owner checked the New style in the day theme; screenshots were captured over ADB. No Spotify crash was recorded.
- BYD keeps its status bar and dock black for Spotify (a firmware list of apps with transparent bars), so the New style blends its rounded corners with black.

## Not yet verified

The update from test.9 to test.10 through the app (feed → download → Android's Update window) has not been seen on a head unit. The New style has not been checked in Arabic (right-to-left) or with right-hand-drive layouts, which keep the Classic player arrangement. The final build was checked in the car in the day theme only. Real vehicle sleep/reboot, account switching A → B → A, offline downloads, steering-wheel controls, DiLink 5.1 / Android 13 and other head units remain unverified.
