🎵 Spotify for Android Head Units — test.7

New: built-in Support and diagnostics!

• Settings → Support → Send diagnostics: preview a technical report and send it directly from the app.
• Optional automatic reports for measured integration failures — at most one new report per day. The first-use checkbox is selected by default; sending starts after Continue. Change your choice anytime in Support.
• Offline queue, delivery status, pending-report cancellation and ZIP export. Manual sending works with automatic reports off.
• Fixed the build/version label in settings.

Reports exclude account data, song/playlist names, location and screenshots. A limited technical summary may appear in our PUBLIC GitHub Issues.
